# Shapat Privacy Policy and Conditions of Use

DRAFT for the test phase. To be reviewed by a lawyer before public release.

Shapat relays are operated by Balkan Bug LLC ("we").

## What we cannot see

All messages, files and calls are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they leave it, with keys that never leave the devices of the people in the conversation. Our relays receive only encrypted data. We cannot read your messages, see your files, or listen to your calls, and we cannot give them to anyone.

Shapat has no accounts and no user identifiers: no phone number, no email, no user name, no user ID. Each conversation uses its own separate message queues, so the relays cannot link your conversations to each other or to you.

## What is stored on your device only

Your profile, your contacts, your groups and your message history are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. If you delete the app or lose your device without an exported backup, this data is lost. We have no copy.

## What our relays store

- Message queues: addresses and keys that let encrypted messages be delivered. A queue is removed when you delete the conversation.
- Undelivered messages: encrypted, kept until delivered, for at most 21 days.
- Files: encrypted parts of files, kept for at most 2 days.
- No logs of message content, no logs of IP addresses, no usage statistics.

Our relays see the IP address of a device while it is connected, as any internet server does. Messages are sent through a second relay where possible, so the relay that delivers a message does not learn the sender's IP address.

## Notifications

On iOS, a notification server receives an encrypted signal that a message is waiting, and passes it to Apple to wake the app. The signal contains no message content and no sender.

## Conditions of use

By using Shapat you agree:

- not to use it for illegal content or activity in public groups;
- not to send spam or abuse the relays with automated traffic.

We may block a public group link or a queue on our relays that is reported as abusive. Reports are sent from the reporting user's device with the messages they choose to share; we never see other content.
